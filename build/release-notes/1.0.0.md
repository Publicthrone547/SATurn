SATurn is a menu bar reminder for the SAT on 5 December 2026. Each day is a ring of 100 math questions, 100 English questions or one practice test; SATurn reminds you every hour until you close it, and tells you the latest time you can still start.

**Downloads:** `SATurn-1.0.0-darwin-arm64.dmg` · `SHA256SUMS`

The app is not notarised. On the first launch, macOS will refuse to open it: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.

## In 1.0

- Menu bar ring and count, popover logging, right-click menu, global hotkeys (`⌃⌥1`, `⌃⌥5`, `⌃⌥0`, `⌃⌥Z`, `⌃⌥S`).
- An editable plan to the exam with rest and sick days.
- Hourly reminders with escalation, the point of no return, sessions and promises.
- Notifications you can answer with a button or a number, and an island from the camera notch.
- Celebrations, streak, history, strict mode, launch at login.

## Requirements

macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.

## Tested on

macOS 27.0.1, Apple M3 Max, with an external 3440×1440 display.

- `make check`: the Go tests (the reminder engine against two full-day golden timelines and 31 rule cases, storage, the tray icon) and the frontend tests.
- The bundled app, with a separate data folder and a fast clock: logging, undo, restart, the morning brief and hourly reminders with escalation, completion and the celebration, the island on a display without a notch, strict mode and the bailout phrase.
- Not yet tested with a person at the screen: real notification delivery and its buttons (needs notification permission), launch at login after a real login, the island under the camera notch.

## Known limits

- Not notarised (see above).
- Launch at login is registered when you finish first run with the switch on; the app has to be in Applications for it.
- Reminders reach you only while the Mac is awake.
- Strict mode blocks quitting from inside the app only; a force quit still ends it.
