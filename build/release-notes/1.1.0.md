SATurn is a menu bar reminder for the SAT on 5 December 2026. 1.1 gives it a clearer face and lets you log a question from any app with one key or one link.

**Downloads:** `SATurn-1.1.0-darwin-arm64.dmg` · `SHA256SUMS`

The app is not notarised. On the first launch, macOS will refuse to open it: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**.

## In 1.1

- A new look: graphite, one lime accent, monospaced numbers. The ring is now a tally of 100 cells (one per question, a row per quarter of the day), and a line of the reminder hours shows the latest time you can start and still finish.
- `+1` is the main button and shows its hotkey.
- Hotkeys move to Command and Option (`⌥⌘1`, `⌥⌘5`, `⌥⌘0`, `⌥⌘Z`, `⌥⌘S`) and each can be re-recorded or cleared in Settings → Log from any app. Updating from 1.0 switches to the new keys.
- Links for the Shortcuts app, Raycast, a Stream Deck or Terminal: `saturn://add`, `saturn://add?n=5`, `saturn://undo`, `saturn://popover`, `saturn://open`.
- An entry from a hotkey or a link shows the new count under the notch for a moment.
- Fixed: on a day with a practice test and questions, the hotkeys did nothing until the test was marked done. They now count towards the questions.

## Requirements

macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.

## Tested on

macOS 27.0.1, Apple M3 Max.

- `make check`: the Go tests (the reminder engine, settings, link parsing, storage, the tray icon) and the frontend tests (formatting, hotkey labels, the popover).
- The installed app: all five hotkeys register; `saturn://add` and `saturn://undo` from Terminal change the count and show the island; `saturn://popover` opens the panel.
- Every screen checked in the browser preview with a fake backend: popover (normal, red zone, test day, done), island, Today, Plan, History, Settings.
- Not yet tested with a person at the screen: recording a new hotkey in Settings, a shortcut made in the Shortcuts app.

## Known limits

- Not notarised (see above).
- Hotkeys with letters may not register while a non-Latin keyboard layout is active; digits and F-keys work in every layout.
- Reminders reach you only while the Mac is awake.
- Strict mode blocks quitting from inside the app only; a force quit still ends it.
