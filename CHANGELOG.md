# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

The first release.

- Menu bar item with a progress ring and today's count; a popover to log questions, start sessions, undo, set an exact value and take a day off.
- A plan from today to the exam, suggested backwards from the exam date and editable day by day: math, English, practice test, combined days, rest and sick.
- Hourly reminders until the day is done, with catch-up after sleep, quiet rules (sessions, recent progress, away from the Mac), escalation, the point of no return and "Later" as a promise.
- Notifications with buttons and a typed reply; an island that drops from the camera notch.
- Ring-lock celebration with confetti and sound; quarter milestones; streak and history.
- Global hotkeys, strict mode, launch at login, first-run setup, exam-day and farewell screens.
