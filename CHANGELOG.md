# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-24

- New look: graphite with one lime accent, monospaced numbers, no display serif. The ring is replaced by a tally of 100 cells (one per question, a row per quarter) and a line of the reminder hours that shows the latest start; the strip and the plan show each day as a filling bar.
- `+1` is the main button in the popover and shows its hotkey.
- Hotkeys can be re-recorded in Settings, one per action, or cleared. The defaults move to Command and Option: `⌥⌘1`, `⌥⌘5`, `⌥⌘0`, `⌥⌘Z`, `⌥⌘S`.
- On a day with a practice test and questions, a hotkey or a link adds to the questions while the test is still open; before, it did nothing.
- `saturn://` links (`saturn://add`, `saturn://add?n=5`, `saturn://undo`, `saturn://popover`, `saturn://open`) for the Shortcuts app, Raycast, a Stream Deck or Terminal.
- An entry from a hotkey or a link shows the new count under the notch for a moment.
- The plan's `Suggest a plan` button is gone; it did nothing that the plan does not already do by itself.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

The first release.

- Menu bar item with a progress ring and today's count; a popover to log questions, start sessions, undo, set an exact value and take a day off.
- A plan from today to the exam, suggested backwards from the exam date and editable day by day: math, English, practice test, combined days, rest and sick.
- Hourly reminders until the day is done, with catch-up after sleep, quiet rules (sessions, recent progress, away from the Mac), escalation, the point of no return and "Later" as a promise.
- Notifications with buttons and a typed reply; an island that drops from the camera notch.
- Ring-lock celebration with confetti and sound; quarter milestones; streak and history.
- Global hotkeys, strict mode, launch at login, first-run setup, exam-day and farewell screens.
